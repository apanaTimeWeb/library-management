# Student Module Forbidden Boundaries

1. **NO ADMIN/MANAGER ENDPOINTS**: Students can only access their own data. Never call endpoints meant for admins, managers, or superadmins.
2. **NO MUTATION OF CORE RECORDS**: Students cannot modify their fee structures, attendance records, or seat assignments directly. They can only submit requests or view data.
3. **NO DASHBOARD LEAKAGE**: Do not use or import KPIs or dashboard components meant for staff roles.
